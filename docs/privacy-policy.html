Game Console Alpha Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Game Console Alpha is a desktop app for Windows, made by Giksaw Interactive (Gladson Mathew), that gathers the games installed on your PC into one library. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and what stays on your PC.

Short version: Game Console Alpha has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not run any servers and never receives your data. Your library and settings stay on your PC. To show cover art, the app asks a few public services for images, and those requests include game names.

Information that stays on your PC

To find your games, Game Console Alpha reads files and Windows registry entries left by game launchers you have installed (such as Steam, Epic Games, GOG Galaxy, Battle.net, Ubisoft Connect, EA app, Xbox, Amazon Games, itch.io and Rockstar Games). It only reads them; it does not change them.

The app saves the following on your PC, inside your Windows user profile:

  • the list of games it found, their install folders and sizes;
  • your favorites, hidden games, custom games you added, launch options and cover-art choices;
  • when you last launched each game from the app;
  • your settings, including a SteamGridDB API key if you choose to enter one.

None of this is sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app does not delete it automatically; you can delete the com.giksaw.gameconsolealpha folders in %APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA% to remove it.

Information sent to other services

Game Console Alpha connects to the internet only to find and show cover art. Like any web request, each one reveals your IP address to the service you connect to.

ServiceWhat is sentWhen
Steam store search
store.steampowered.com		 Names of installed games that aren't from Steam Automatically, to find cover art for those games, and when you search for artwork
Steam image servers
steamstatic.com		 Steam app IDs of games in your library (as image requests) Whenever cover art is shown
SteamGridDB
steamgriddb.com		 Game names and your SteamGridDB API key Only if you enter your own API key in Settings
Other image hosts A request for the image When showing cover art provided by a launcher (for example Epic Games or itch.io) or an image URL you paste yourself
Google Search, in your web browser The game's name Only when you click "Search the web" for cover art

These services handle that information under their own privacy policies. Games, launchers and websites that you open from Game Console Alpha are separate programs with their own privacy practices.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store or sell personal information.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • We don't require an account or sign-in.

Children

Game Console Alpha doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
