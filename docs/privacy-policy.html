Game Console Alpha Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Game Console Alpha is a desktop app for Windows, made by Giksaw Interactive (Gladson Mathew), that gathers the games installed on your PC into one library, and can list games you own in Steam or Epic Games that aren't installed yet. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and what stays on your PC.

Short version: Game Console Alpha has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not run any servers and never receives your data. Your library and settings stay on your PC. To show cover art, the app asks a few public services for images, and those requests include game names. The Trending page gets its lists and search results from the Steam store.

Information that stays on your PC

To find your games, Game Console Alpha reads files and Windows registry entries left by game launchers you have installed (such as Steam, Epic Games, GOG Galaxy, Battle.net, Ubisoft Connect, EA app, Xbox, Amazon Games, itch.io and Rockstar Games). For Steam and Epic Games it also reads the launcher's own local record of the games you own, so it can list ones that aren't installed. It only reads these files; it does not change them, and it never signs in to your accounts.

When you click Install, Game Console Alpha asks Steam or Epic Games to install the game. The launcher shows its own install window and does the download; Game Console Alpha follows its progress by reading the launcher's local files.

The app saves the following on your PC, inside your Windows user profile:

  • the list of games it found, their install folders and sizes;
  • your favorites, hidden games, custom games you added, launch options and cover-art choices;
  • when you last launched each game from the app;
  • your settings, including a SteamGridDB API key if you choose to enter one.

None of this is sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app does not delete it automatically; you can delete the com.giksaw.gameconsolealpha folders in %APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA% to remove it.

Game Console Alpha connects to the internet only to find and show cover art, and to show the Trending page. Like any web request, each one reveals your IP address to the service you connect to.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store or sell personal information.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • We don't require an account or sign-in.

Children

Game Console Alpha doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
